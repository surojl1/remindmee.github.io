Answers to common questions.

If you’re new to RemindMe, start with the basics below. You can also browse the app overview.

How do I add a task or reminder?

Open the relevant tab and list, then tap + to add a task. Type or dictate its title. If the text includes a recognized time phrase, such as “tomorrow at 3pm” or “in 2 hours,” RemindMe shows the interpreted reminder time before you save. To choose another time, turn on “Set time manually.”

How do I organize tasks into tabs?

Open Settings from a tab to rename it, choose an icon, or add another tab. Name tabs for any area of life, such as Home, Work, or Projects. The To-Do tab brings tasks together and lets you search, sort, and filter them.

Why didn’t my typed or spoken time become a reminder?

RemindMe sets a reminder when it recognizes a supported date or time phrase. Try a clear phrase such as “in 30 minutes,” “tomorrow at 9am,” “next Monday,” or “every weekday at 8am.” Check the interpreted time in the add screen, or set a time manually.

Voice input isn’t working. What should I check?

Voice input needs microphone and speech recognition access. On your iPhone, open Settings and check RemindMe’s permissions. Also make sure speech recognition is available for your device and language. You can always type the task instead.

Why didn’t I receive a notification?

Check that notifications are allowed for RemindMe in iPhone Settings, and that the task has a future reminder time. Reminders during configured quiet hours are delayed until quiet hours end; high-priority reminders can still come through.

How do snooze and repeat alerts work?

Use Done or a snooze option on a reminder notification, or snooze a task from its menu. In Settings, choose how many repeat alerts to schedule and the interval between them. A repeating task advances to its next occurrence when completed.

How do I change reminder timing or quiet hours?

Open Settings from a tab. There you can adjust repeat alerts, the default morning time used for phrases like “tomorrow,” and optional quiet hours. Save changes with Done.

Why isn’t my widget up to date?

Open RemindMe to refresh the upcoming reminder list, then give iOS a moment to update the widget. iOS controls how often widgets refresh, so an update may not appear immediately.

How do I use Siri or add a reminder to Calendar?

RemindMe includes an Add Reminder Siri Shortcut. You can also choose Add to Calendar from a task’s menu when it has a reminder time. Calendar access may be required to create the event.
