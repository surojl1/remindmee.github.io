A flexible way to stay organized

Make RemindMe fit your life.

Build separate spaces for the things you do, then bring all your tasks together when you need the full picture.

01

Separate tabs for every area

Create and name tabs for work, home, projects, clients, or anything else. Keep each area organized, with the option to group tasks by person when that helps.

02

Turn words into reminders

Type or dictate a task with a time phrase like “tomorrow at 3” or “in 2 hours.” RemindMe recognizes the date and time and sets the reminder for you. You can also choose a time manually.

03

See every to-do together

Use the To-Do view to search across your tasks, switch between open and completed items, sort by time or priority, and filter by tag or list.

04

Stay on top of details

Break tasks into subtasks, add tags and priority, and set reminders to repeat daily, on weekdays, weekly, or monthly.

05

Reminders that follow through

Get repeat alerts, set quiet hours, and mark a task done or snooze it right from its notification. High-priority reminders can break through quiet hours.

06

Quick ways to capture and check in

Add reminders with Siri Shortcuts, see what’s next on Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, or add a timed reminder to your calendar.

07

Bring in people when useful

Keep contact details and related follow-ups together. Add people from selected contacts or scan a photo of a name list.

Capture it now. Get reminded when it matters.

Plan your day, keep projects moving, remember an errand, or follow up with someone. RemindMe gives every task a place—and helps bring it back at the right time.
